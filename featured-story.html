One Day.
Three Moments.

Three photographs, three fragments of a day, held together by feeling rather than sequence.

The Day Between Moments

The quiet
between
frames.

Some days are remembered for the big moment. Others stay with us because of everything that happened around it.

The quiet glance before the music begins. A hand reaching for another hand. The laughter that arrives unexpectedly. The little pauses no one planned for, but somehow become the moments that stay.

This feature is a small visual essay built from three photographs. There is no strict beginning, middle or end — only three fragments connected by feeling.

Fragments of a day.

FOLIA is an archive of moments that deserve to be revisited slowly. This is one of them.
